# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This guide gives a quick overview of the project management approach used at OctoAcme. Projects move through a repeatable lifecycle that begins with initiation and planning, flows into iterative execution and tracking, and closes with release and retrospective. The goal is to keep teams aligned on outcomes, deliver value in a predictable way, and improve continuously through lessons learned.

## Project management lifecycle

OctoAcme's project process is designed to help teams turn strategy into delivery while managing risk, dependencies, and stakeholder expectations.

1. Initiation
   - Align stakeholders, define the problem or opportunity, and confirm measurable outcomes.
   - Document scope, success criteria, and the initial project charter.

2. Planning
   - Prioritize the work, define milestones, and create a clear delivery plan.
   - Identify dependencies, risks, and resource needs before execution begins.

3. Execution and tracking
   - Execute in regular team rhythms with progress tracking, backlog refinement, and status communication.
   - Maintain focus on outcomes while adjusting to changes in scope, risks, or priorities.

4. Risk management and communication
   - Monitor risks, dependencies, and issues as they emerge.
   - Communicate status, decisions, and escalations to the right stakeholders at the right time.

5. Release and deployment
   - Validate quality before release, coordinate deployment readiness, and confirm stakeholder acceptance.
   - Monitor release health and address issues quickly if they appear.

6. Retrospective and continuous improvement
   - Capture learnings from delivery, reflect on what worked, and define follow-up improvements.
   - Feed those lessons back into future planning and execution.

The Product Manager owns outcomes and prioritization, while the Project Manager coordinates delivery, risk management, and communication. Developers, QA, and stakeholders all contribute to delivery and validation across the lifecycle.
